Artist bio

Real life heroes

Real life heroes exist. Sometimes they leap right from comic books and into our lives. They tend to show up right when they are needed most, when the night is darkest.

The rap duo 8 Ton Grams (known individually as 8tahn and GRAEME) features two such heroes. Both embody the grit, grind, and creativity that make indie hip hop so affecting, but also the light, magic, and heart that can only be the product of a true twinning of souls, and in this case bodies, in the form of a real life organ donation and a life saving friendship.

Their story is out there if you care to find it, left in musical tales around the internet by the duo's various solo incarnations (8tahn, GRAEME, No1s Ethan). As 8 Ton Grams the duo will work to share their epic adventure with their broadest audience yet.

It's a story of friendship, compassion, dedication, sacrifice, and humanity. But lest we forget to mention:

THIS ISH BUMPS.

Flint and Detroit still got something to say, and this is "just a prelude."
